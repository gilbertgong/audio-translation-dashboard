Capture audio

Capture translates sound from your computer instead of the microphone: a video, a meeting, anything that's playing.

It uses the browser's screen sharing, the same one meeting apps use to share a tab or your screen, in reverse: you share with this page, and it listens to the sound. In the browser's picker, choose:

  • A browser tab (best), with Also share tab audio on: just that tab's sound, e.g. a YouTube video or a meeting in the browser. Mute tab then silences it for you while it's still captured, so you hear only the translations.
  • Entire screen, with Also share system audio on: everything your computer plays (Windows), e.g. a meeting app. This includes the spoken translations.
  • A window shares no sound in most browsers.

The picture isn't used (it's kept tiny). Phrases are split at pauses, as in Continuous Audio Mode. If the sound is stereo, translation starts with the first channel that has sound; click a channel's meter to choose the other. Stop with the button, or with the browser's own "Stop sharing".
